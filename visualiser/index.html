Visualiser

  1. Paste a YouTube or YouTube Music playlist link above and press Load.
  2. Press Capture tab audio, choose this tab, and make sure Also share tab audio is ticked.
  3. Pick a visual, hit F for fullscreen, and leave the mouse alone.

Works in Chrome and Edge. YouTube won't let pages read its audio directly, so the visualiser listens to the tab instead.
